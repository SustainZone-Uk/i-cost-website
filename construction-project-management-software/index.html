The problem

Five tools, five versions
of the same job.

On most jobs the estimate lives in a spreadsheet, quotes in email, orders in Word, hours on paper and the programme in another app. Nobody sees the whole picture until the final account.

  • The estimate is in one spreadsheet, the budget in another, and they drift apart.

    The estimate is locked as the approved budget, and every version is kept.

  • Supplier quotes, orders and deliveries are spread across inboxes.

    Requests, quotes, orders and deliveries sit on the project, item by item.

  • Site hours arrive on paper at the end of the week.

    Hours are logged on a phone, approved and costed into the report.

  • Nobody knows the forecast margin until the job is finished.

    Committed cost and forecast margin update as orders, invoices and hours come in.

  • The programme is in a separate tool nobody updates.

    Dates and progress sit next to the estimate, with late work flagged.

  • New starters don't know where anything is.

    Every project follows the same eight steps, and the project page shows what to do next.

Questions

Project management questions

Something else? Ask us.

Is I-Cost construction project management software?

Yes, for the commercial side of construction projects: estimating, client quotations, supplier management, purchase orders, deliveries, timesheets, the programme, cost tracking and carbon. It does not replace drawing management, RFI or snagging tools.

Is I-Cost suitable for small construction businesses?

Yes. You pay £35 per user per month with no set-up fee, so a team of two costs £70 a month, and you can start with a 7-day free trial on one live project.

Does it replace Microsoft Project or other planning tools?

I-Cost includes a simple programme (Gantt chart) built from the estimate, with dates, progress and late work flagged. For detailed critical-path scheduling you may still want a dedicated planning tool.

Can site teams use it on their phones?

Yes. I-Cost runs in the browser and installs like an app on phone, tablet and PC. Site teams log hours and confirm deliveries from their phone.

How long does it take to get started?

You can sign up, import a BOQ, invite your team and send your first quotation request on the first day, with a guided tutorial showing each step on the real screens.
