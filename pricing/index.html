How pricing works

Simple, per user,
month to month.

  • Pay for your team

    Each person in your company with their own login is one user at £35 a month. A team of five is £175 a month.

  • Suppliers are free

    Suppliers receive requests, send prices and manage orders and deliveries in the portal without paying anything.

  • No lock-in

    Billing is monthly and managed by you in the app. Cancel at any time; access runs to the end of the month already paid for.

  • Try it first

    Seven days of the full platform with your own BOQ and suppliers, and no bank card needed to start.

  • Longer terms

    Prefer a 12 or 24 month contract, or have a larger team? Request a quote and we will come back to you.

  • Help to get started

    Ask in your quote for help importing existing BOQs, price lists and suppliers, or for training your team.

Questions

Pricing questions

Something else? Ask us.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You get 7 days of the full platform, and you do not need a bank card to start.

Who counts as a user?

Each person in your company with their own login is one user. Suppliers using the supplier portal are free.

Are the beta features included?

Yes. Every feature is in the one plan, including BIM model import and whole life carbon assessments, which are in beta.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Billing is managed in the app through Stripe, where you can change your card, download invoices or cancel. A cancelled subscription stops at the end of the month already paid for, with no further charges.

Do you offer longer contracts or help getting set up?

Yes. For 12 or 24 month terms, larger teams, help importing your existing BOQs, price lists and suppliers, or training for your team, request a quote and we will come back to you.

Is support included?

Yes. Every plan includes the guided tutorial, Costa the in-app help assistant, and the Help Desk to reach the I-Cost team.

Is I-Cost suitable for small construction businesses?

Yes. You pay £35 per user per month with no set-up fee, so a team of two costs £70 a month, and you can start with a 7-day free trial on one live project. Suppliers use the portal free.
