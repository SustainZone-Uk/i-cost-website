Up and runningon day one.

Getting started

Five steps to your
first supplier prices.

  1. Start the free trial

    Sign up with your work email for 7 days of the full platform. No bank card needed.

  2. Add your team

    Invite colleagues by email and give each a role. They choose their own password.

  3. Create a project

    Add the client, dates and margin, and give the right people access. Or keep the demo project to explore.

  4. Import the BOQ

    Upload the client's Excel or CSV BOQ, or an IFC model (beta), check the total and save it to the estimate.

  5. Ask suppliers for prices

    Pick items from the estimate and send one request to several suppliers. They reply in the free portal.

Every project

How to manage a construction project,
step by step.

The same eight steps sit across the top of your dashboard, and each project page shows where it stands and what to do next.

  1. Project

    Client, dates, margin, team

    Set up the job and give your team access.

  2. Estimate

    BOQ import, pricing, carbon

    Price every item at cost and selling rates, with carbon on each line.

  3. Secure work

    Quotation, discount, acceptance

    Send the client a quotation and record the agreed contract sum.

  4. Budget

    Lock the estimate as budget

    The locked estimate becomes the approved budget everything is measured against.

  5. Procurement

    RFQs, orders, deliveries

    Ask suppliers for prices, order from the best and record what arrives.

  6. People & time

    Timesheets, approvals

    Staff and site hours logged, approved and costed.

  7. Progress

    Programme, site progress

    Dates and progress for each section, with late work flagged.

  8. Report

    Cost, margin, forecast

    Committed cost, forecast final cost and forecast margin, ready to share.

On site and in the office

Install it like an app.

I-Cost runs in the browser and installs on phone, tablet and PC, with no app store and nothing to download. It is always the same live system.

  • iPhone and iPad

    Open I-Cost in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

  • Android

    Open I-Cost in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. Press and hold the icon for "Log my hours".

  • PC and Mac

    Open I-Cost in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon in the address bar. It opens in its own window.

Your data

Safe, separate and yours.

Each company's data is kept separate, people only see the projects they are given, and roles control what each person can change. Everything is backed up off-site every hour, with a standby server ready, and you can download all your company's data at any time.

  • Roles and per-person permissions
  • Read-only Reviewer access
  • Hourly off-site backups
  • Standby server ready to take over
  • Download your data at any time (UK GDPR)
  • Billing managed by you, cancel any time

Questions

Getting started questions

Something else? Ask us.

Do we need training?

Most people start with the guided tutorial inside I-Cost, which walks through each step on the real buttons, and the demo project you can keep or remove. Costa, the in-app help assistant, answers questions, and the Help Desk reaches the I-Cost team.

Can we try it on a live project?

Yes. The 7-day free trial is the full platform, so you can import a real BOQ and invite suppliers. If you would rather start with a guided commercial health check or a pilot on one project, book a demo.

What happens after the trial?

You subscribe by card at £35 per user per month. Billing is managed in the app through Stripe, where you can change your card, download invoices or cancel; a cancelled subscription stops at the end of the month already paid for.

Is our data safe?

Each company's data is kept separate, people only see the projects they are given, and data is backed up off-site every hour with a standby server ready. You can download all your company's data at any time.

How do I manage a construction project in I-Cost, step by step?

Set up the project with its client, dates, margin and team; import the BOQ and price the estimate; send the client quotation and record the agreed sum; lock the estimate as the budget; ask suppliers for prices and raise purchase orders; log and approve hours; date the programme and track progress; then report on cost, forecast margin and carbon.
