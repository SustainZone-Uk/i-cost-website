Questions

Purchase order questions

Something else? Ask us.

Can I still print and email purchase orders?

Yes. Each order prints as a tidy PO document or PDF and is emailed to the supplier, who can also see it in the free portal.

When does an order count as committed?

When it is sent. Drafts don't count, so you can prepare orders without changing the report. Committed cost per order is the order value or the invoices, whichever is higher.

Can I record part deliveries?

Yes. Record what arrived on each line, as many times as needed, or confirm a supplier's dispatch and mark anything short, damaged or wrong.

Do suppliers need an I-Cost account?

They receive the order by email, and can use the free supplier portal to accept it, record dispatches and print QR delivery notes.
