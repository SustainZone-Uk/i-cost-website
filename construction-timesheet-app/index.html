The problem

Paper timesheets
cost more than paper.

When hours arrive late and on paper, labour overspend is found weeks after it happened.

  • Paper sheets arrive at the end of the week, or later.

    Hours are logged on the day, on a phone.

  • Filling in a timesheet takes too long on site.

    Tap the item, tap Full day or Morning, save.

Questions

Timesheet questions

Something else? Ask us.

Does the timesheet app work offline?

No. I-Cost needs a mobile or Wi-Fi connection to save hours, so they reach the office straight away.

Do I need to download an app?

No app store needed. Open I-Cost in the phone's browser and add it to the home screen; it then opens like an app, with a "Log my hours" shortcut.

Who can approve timesheets?

Company admins approve everything; project managers, project leads, site managers and supervisors approve on the projects they are on. Nobody can approve their own hours.

Can a supervisor log hours for the whole gang?

Yes. The people from the supervisor's last timesheet are ticked already, one tap sets everyone's hours, and each person's hours can still be changed.
