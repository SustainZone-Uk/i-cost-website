The intelligence layer

I-Cost checks the numbers
while you work.

Not a chatbot guessing prices. These checks run on your own data: your orders, your suppliers and your estimates.

    Copied an old estimate? It checks what you paid since.

    When you paste rows from another project, I-Cost looks up your purchase orders for those items. Anything bought at a different price is listed with the old rate, the latest order rate and the change, and the cost before and after. Keep the old rate, take the new one or type your own.

    Who should price this? It suggests suppliers.

    I-Cost reads the items in a quotation request and ranks your suppliers by how many of them each is likely to price, from what they supply, what you have ordered from them before and how often they reply.

    Each estimate line is matched to a carbon factor by its description. You choose how strict the match is, and anything I-Cost is unsure about waits for you to confirm.

Works with Xero

Supplier invoices into Xero,
without retyping.

Two ways, and you choose. Automatic: the live Xero connection sends approved supplier invoices to Xero as draft bills, with one click or by themselves as each one is approved, and brings the paid date back into I-Cost. Manual: download a file in Xero's bill import layout and import it yourself, free.

The live connection is £10 a month for your whole company, on top of your users. Ask for it from inside I-Cost and we switch it on; your admin then connects your own Xero.

  • Draft bills only: I-Cost never approves, pays or deletes anything in Xero
  • Suppliers become Xero contacts and projects become tracking
  • Paid dates come back to the order, the Item tracker and the supplier portal
  • Suppliers can get an email when their invoice is paid, in your company's name
  • Your own Xero sign-in: I-Cost never sees your Xero password
  1. Ask usIn I-Cost, Purchase orders > Export bills for Xero or My Account > Connected apps: press Ask us to switch it on.
  2. Connect onceYour admin presses Connect to Xero, signs in to Xero, picks the organisation and allows access.
  3. SendTick approved invoices and press Send to Xero, or let each one go by itself when it is approved.
  4. Paid comes backYour finance team approves and pays in Xero as usual. The paid date shows in I-Cost.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Ask us.

What is I-Cost?

I-Cost is construction estimating, procurement, cost control and carbon software for UK contractors. The client's BOQ, your estimate, supplier quotes, purchase orders, site hours, programme and reports sit in one place, so you can see the forecast margin on every project.

Who is I-Cost for?

UK main contractors and specialist subcontractors: commercial directors, quantity surveyors, estimators, buyers, project and site managers, sustainability leads and finance teams. Suppliers use the supplier portal free of charge.

Can I import my client's Excel BOQ?

Yes. Upload the Excel or CSV bill of quantities, tell I-Cost what each column is, check the preview and the total, and bring the lines straight into your estimate. Nobody has to retype it.

Can I import a BIM model?

Yes, in beta. Choose an IFC file exported from Revit, ArchiCAD, Tekla or a similar program. I-Cost adds up the quantities in your browser and shows them in the same preview as a BOQ import. The model itself is not uploaded to our server. Working in Revit 2023 to 2027? The free I-Cost for Revit add-in sends the quantities straight from Revit, with no IFC export.

Can't I just ask an AI chatbot for prices?

An AI chatbot can help draft an estimate, but it does not know today's prices from your suppliers, and material prices move every month. Research has found AI estimates unreliable until they are given real rates. I-Cost keeps your supplier quotes and your own purchase order prices in one place, and checks copied estimates against what you last paid.

Does I-Cost work out embodied carbon?

Yes. Each estimate line can carry a carbon factor, from UK ICE data or your own EPD figures. Whole life carbon assessments (beta) cover life-cycle stages A1 to A5 through to D, following the RICS Professional Standard (2nd edition) and BS EN 15978.

Can I use I-Cost on my phone?

Yes. I-Cost runs in the browser and installs like an app on phone, tablet and PC, with no app store. Site teams log their hours from their phone with a Log my hours shortcut.

Does I-Cost work with Xero?

Yes. The live Xero connection sends supplier invoices to Xero as draft bills and brings paid dates back, for £10 a month for your whole company; ask for it from inside I-Cost. Or download a file for Xero's bill import, free. QuickBooks and Sage aren't supported yet.

Do suppliers have to pay?

No. Suppliers get a free portal where they receive your quotation requests, send prices and follow your orders.

How much does I-Cost cost?

£35 per user per month after a 7-day free trial. No bank card is needed to start the trial. See pricing

Can I manage my whole construction project in I-Cost?

You can run the commercial side of a construction project in one place: the client's BOQ and estimate, the client quotation, supplier quotes, purchase orders and deliveries, site hours, the programme, and cost and carbon reports. I-Cost does not replace drawing management, RFI or snagging tools.

Is I-Cost suitable for small construction businesses?

Yes. You pay £35 per user per month with no set-up fee, so a team of two costs £70 a month, and you can start with a 7-day free trial on one live project. Suppliers use the portal free.

How is I-Cost better than a construction cost tracking spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet only knows what someone typed into it. In I-Cost, orders, invoices, deliveries and site hours update the committed cost and the forecast margin as they happen, every figure links to the records behind it, and nothing is copied between files.
