Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else? Ask us.

What is I-Cost?

I-Cost is construction estimating, procurement, cost control and carbon software for UK contractors. The client's BOQ, your estimate, supplier quotes, purchase orders, site hours, programme and reports sit in one place, so you can see the forecast margin on every project.

Who is I-Cost for?

UK main contractors and specialist subcontractors: commercial directors, quantity surveyors, estimators, buyers, project and site managers, sustainability leads and finance teams. Suppliers use the supplier portal free of charge.

Can I import my client's Excel BOQ?

Yes. Upload the Excel or CSV bill of quantities, tell I-Cost what each column is, check the preview and the total, and bring the lines straight into your estimate. Nobody has to retype it.

Can I import a BIM model?

Yes, in beta. Choose an IFC file exported from Revit, ArchiCAD, Tekla or a similar program. I-Cost adds up the quantities in your browser and shows them in the same preview as a BOQ import. The model itself is not uploaded to our server.

Can't I just ask an AI chatbot for prices?

An AI chatbot can help draft an estimate, but it does not know today's prices from your suppliers, and material prices move every month. Research has found AI estimates unreliable until they are given real rates. I-Cost keeps your supplier quotes and your own purchase order prices in one place, and checks copied estimates against what you last paid.

Does I-Cost work out embodied carbon?

Yes. Each estimate line can carry a carbon factor, from UK ICE data or your own EPD figures. Whole life carbon assessments (beta) cover life-cycle stages A1 to A5 through to D, following the RICS Professional Standard (2nd edition) and BS EN 15978.

Can I use I-Cost on my phone?

Yes. I-Cost runs in the browser and installs like an app on phone, tablet and PC, with no app store. Site teams log their hours from their phone with a Log my hours shortcut.

Do suppliers have to pay?

No. Suppliers get a free portal where they receive your quotation requests, send prices and follow your orders.

How much does I-Cost cost?

£35 per user per month after a 7-day free trial. No bank card is needed to start the trial. See pricing

Can I manage my whole construction project in I-Cost?

You can run the commercial side of a construction project in one place: the client's BOQ and estimate, the client quotation, supplier quotes, purchase orders and deliveries, site hours, the programme, and cost and carbon reports. I-Cost does not replace drawing management, RFI or snagging tools.

Is I-Cost suitable for small construction businesses?

Yes. You pay £35 per user per month with no set-up fee, so a team of two costs £70 a month, and you can start with a 7-day free trial on one live project. Suppliers use the portal free.

How is I-Cost better than a construction cost tracking spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet only knows what someone typed into it. In I-Cost, orders, invoices, deliveries and site hours update the committed cost and the forecast margin as they happen, every figure links to the records behind it, and nothing is copied between files.
