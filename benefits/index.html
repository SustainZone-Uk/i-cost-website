Where margin leaks

Six leaks.
Six fixes.

The same problems come up on almost every job. Here is what changes when the job sits in one place.

  • Quantities are retyped from the client's BOQ, and a line or a decimal goes missing.

    The BOQ is imported, checked against its total and priced in one estimate.

  • Rates are copied from an old job, a spreadsheet or an AI chatbot, and nobody knows they are out of date.

    Prices come from supplier quotes and your own orders, and copied rates are checked against what you last paid.

  • The client agrees a discount, but the reports still use the original price.

    The accepted quotation sets the agreed contract sum used in every report.

  • Orders go out without anyone comparing them to the budget.

    Every order counts against the approved budget the moment it is sent, by area and cost type.

  • Deliveries arrive short or damaged, and invoices are paid in full anyway.

    Site confirms what arrived; goods received, invoices and credit notes sit on the order.

  • Labour hours reach the office weeks late, so overspend is found too late.

    Hours are logged on a phone, approved and costed into the report the same week.

Why guessing costs so much

AI can draft an estimate.
It can't price your job.

AI tools are useful for drafting, but they don't know today's prices from your suppliers. Real prices come from your suppliers' quotes and your own orders, and with margins this thin, one wrong guess can cost the whole profit.

Questions

Benefits questions

Something else? Ask us.

How does I-Cost protect margin?

By keeping the agreed contract sum, the approved budget, supplier quotes, orders, invoices, deliveries and site hours in one place, so the forecast margin is always up to date and problems show up while there is still time to act.

Can an AI chatbot price our job instead?

An AI chatbot can help draft an estimate, but it does not know today's prices from your suppliers. A 2024 university study found GPT-4's estimates were inaccurate and inconsistent until it was given real labour, plant and material rates. I-Cost keeps those real rates: your supplier quotes and your own purchase order prices.

Does I-Cost guarantee a saving?

No software can guarantee a saving. I-Cost gives you the figures early enough to act on them: what has been committed against the budget, what the forecast margin is, and which items are still waiting for prices or deliveries.

What does it cost?

£35 per user per month after a 7-day free trial, with no bank card needed to start. Suppliers using the portal do not pay. See pricing

How is I-Cost better than a construction cost tracking spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet only knows what someone typed into it. In I-Cost, orders, invoices, deliveries and site hours update the committed cost and the forecast margin as they happen, every figure links to the records behind it, and nothing is copied between files.
