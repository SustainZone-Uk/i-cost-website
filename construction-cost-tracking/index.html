Step by step

How the forecast margin
is worked out.

  1. Contract value

    The agreed sum from the accepted client quotation.

  2. Approved budget

    The estimate at cost, locked as the budget, by area.

  3. Committed

    For each order, the order value or its invoices, whichever is higher, plus staff and site labour hours at their rates.

  4. Forecast

    Each area at its budget, or at what is committed if that is more.

  5. Margin

    Contract value minus forecast final cost, in pounds and per cent.

Questions

Cost tracking questions

Something else? Ask us.

How is committed cost calculated?

For each sent purchase order, the order value or its invoices, whichever is higher, plus staff hours at their cost rates and site labour at the operatives' rates, with approved hours shown alongside. A closed order counts only what was invoiced.

Can I export cost reports to Excel?

Yes. Reports download as CSV for Excel, and the project report can also be customised and downloaded as a Word or PDF document with your logo.

Are figures shown before VAT?

Yes. The project cost report shows all amounts before VAT.

How is I-Cost better than a construction cost tracking spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet only knows what someone typed into it. In I-Cost, orders, invoices, deliveries and site hours update the committed cost and forecast margin as they happen, and every figure links to the records behind it.
