Questions

Who uses I-Cost?

Something else? Ask us.

Is I-Cost quantity surveying software?

I-Cost covers the quantity surveyor's day-to-day commercial work on a contractor's side: pricing from the BOQ, the approved budget, committed cost, cost to complete and forecast margin, with the records behind every figure. Valuations, payment applications and a variations register are not in I-Cost yet.

Is I-Cost only for main contractors?

No. It is used by main contractors and by specialist and trade subcontractors who price, buy and deliver their own packages. Suppliers use the free supplier portal, and a supplier who also runs jobs can switch to their own company with one login.

Can each person see only what they need?

Yes. Roles and permissions control what each person can see and change, people are added to the projects they work on, and the read-only Reviewer role lets directors, client QSs or auditors look without changing anything.

How much does it cost?

£35 per user per month after a 7-day free trial, with no bank card needed to start. Suppliers using the portal do not pay. See pricing

Is I-Cost suitable for small construction businesses?

Yes. You pay £35 per user per month with no set-up fee, so a team of two costs £70 a month, and you can start with a 7-day free trial on one live project. Suppliers use the portal free.

Can I manage suppliers and subcontractors in I-Cost?

Yes. Suppliers and subcontractors receive your quotation requests and purchase orders in a free portal, send prices, accept orders and record deliveries, and I-Cost suggests who to ask from what they supply. Your own site labour is tracked through timesheets.
